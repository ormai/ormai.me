About me

Currently, I'm pursuing my master's degree in computer science. That takes most of my time. What remains I spend bringing down my to-read pile, and fiddling with personal coding projects.

The coding projects I'm proudest of are pinned on my GitHub profile. Some of them are university group projects. I dedicated a lot of time on each of them, to make them beautiful and usable, to make them just right. I find satisfaction in that, coding becomes like woodcraft.

I've also a latent interest in Rust. I feel realized to the core when my code is compiled to assembly and gobbled up directly by the CPU, no interpretation, no bytecode virtual machines, only bare, overheated metal.

As for my to-read pile, I track my progress on StoryGraph. I read anything, even though I love a good fantasy or a clever science fiction premise to a story. I'm careful not to reduce reading to a chore, so I don't care about challenges, and I'm in fact a rather slow reader.
